# UMVC3 STEAM ROLLBACK
**made by itsnotbloodborne**

I love Marvel. I think it's the best fighting game ever made, and so do my friends. Unfortunately, we're separated by an entire continent. Marvel for us was simply unplayable.

It bothered me so much that I've committed roughly $1,000 of my own money and several hundred hours of cumulative time between development, iteration, playtesting, and the help of my friends to bring seamless rollback to the game while preserving the ease of playing through Steam.

I proudly present **Ultimate Marvel vs. Capcom 3 - Steam Rollback**.

## Play Marvel Through Steam

With this mod, you won't jump through any new hoops. You'll simply play Marvel as normal, through Steam.

Want to play ranked? Fire up the ranked options to find another player using the mod.

Rather do a custom lobby? Go ahead. Player matches? Covered. Bring your friends into a lobby to play together, spectate, and rotate through matches.

Use the normal Steam invitations or the in-game search. Everyone playing together should install the same version of the mod.

## Installation

1. Download the latest ZIP from [Releases](https://github.com/ItsNotBloodborne/umvc3-rollback-releases/releases/latest) and extract it.
2. Open `Umvc3RollbackSetup.exe`, select your UMVC3 installation, and click **Install / Update**.
3. Launch using the **UMVC3 Rollback** shortcut added to your desktop, or run `Umvc3Rollback.exe` in your game folder.

Want Steam to launch rollback whenever you press Play? Click **Add to Steam Play button** in the installer. If Steam is running, the installer will ask to close it and reopen it afterward. You can switch back with **Remove from Steam Play button**.

## Updates

The rollback launcher checks for a newer version before starting the game. When an update is available, it asks whether you'd like to install it. Choose Yes to download the update, run the installer automatically, and relaunch. I recommend keeping it up to date.

> While this repository is private for update testing, automatic update checks cannot access its releases on ordinary installations. Public update delivery will become available when the repository is made public.

## Playing and Reporting Issues

The goal is for playing with rollback to feel as seamless as playing the game normally. You may experience an occasional crash, but overall, you should have a very smooth time playing.

We've spent a lot of time stress-testing under deliberately poor network conditions, including added latency, jitter, and packet loss. No connection is identical, so reports from your matches help us keep improving it.

If you get a crash, please open the installer and use **Export Logs**. The ZIP should appear on your desktop. Share it in the project's Discord with a short description of what happened, which match mode you were using, and whether one or both players crashed.

The Discord invite will be added here when it's ready.
